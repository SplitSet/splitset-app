## 👥 **Client Onboarding Process**

### **For Each New Store Owner**
1. **They visit**: `https://your-domain.com/register`
2. **Step 1**: Personal details (name, email, password)
3. **Step 2**: Shopify store setup:
   - Shop domain: `their-store.myshopify.com`
   - Access token: From their Shopify Custom App
4. **Result**: Instant access to their private dashboard

### **What They Need to Prepare**
1. **Create Custom App** in their Shopify admin:
   - Settings → Apps → Develop apps → Create app
   - Enable scopes: `read_products`, `write_products`, `read_orders`
   - Install app and copy access token

2. **Register** on your platform with their store details

3. **Start using** the SplitSet app immediately

### **Common Issues & Solutions**
1. **Database connection failed**: Check DATABASE_URL and PostgreSQL status
2. **Redis connection failed**: Verify Redis credentials and connectivity
3. **Authentication errors**: Check JWT_SECRET and ENCRYPTION_KEY
4. **High memory usage**: Restart app container or scale vertically
